# PTEC - Water Dispensing System
The Water Dispensing System is essentially a circuit that will be installed water tanks and will enable the vendor to ensure correct delivery of water to the actual customer and will also measure the amount of water delivered. It will also be responsible to sync with cloud for customer verification and water dispensing statuses.
